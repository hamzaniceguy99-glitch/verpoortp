IMPORTANT — ASBESTOS BEFORE INSULATION: insulation work on roofs, lofts, cavity walls and technical spaces in buildings constructed before 2001 frequently disturbs material containing asbestos, including roof sheeting, insulation board, pipe lagging and bituminous products. An asbestos inventory is therefore obtained before intrusive work begins, work is STOPPED IMMEDIATELY where material suspected of containing asbestos is encountered, and any removal falling within the licensable categories is referred EXCLUSIVELY to a firm licensed for asbestos removal; the company does not itself remove asbestos. Clients are reminded that in Flanders an asbestos certificate is separately required on the sale of a building constructed before 2001, which is the owner's obligation and not the contractor's.
